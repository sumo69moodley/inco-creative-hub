Good to know

Questions we get asked

Can I hire the studio without a crew?

Yes. Studio-only hire is R999 excl. VAT/hour and includes a set of your choice. You bring your own cameras and operator. A technician is on site to help you get set up.

How many people can the space hold?

The vodcast sets are built for up to four people on camera. For larger shoots, the open floor comfortably takes a crew of twelve plus talent. For events the space comfortably holds up to 100 pax.

How quickly do we get our files?

Photography selects land in an online gallery the same day, with retouched files in three working days. Video and vodcast first cuts are typically ready within 48 hours. Campaign work is scheduled against an agreed timeline.

Do you shoot outside the studio?

We shoot on location across Durban with no extra travel charge within a 20km radius.

What do we need to bring?

Yourself, your talking points and anything you want on camera — product, wardrobe, branding. Everything else is here: mics, cameras, lights, backdrops, teleprompter, steamer and wifi.

Can you handle the whole content plan, not just the shoot?

We can, using our sister company iNCO Creative, which is a full design and media agency, so strategy, scripting, branding, editing and distribution can all sit with one team.
