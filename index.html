How it works

Four steps, zero admin.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us what you need and who it’s for. A 20-minute call is usually enough to scope it.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We lock the date, the set, the shot list and the crew, then send one clear quote.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    You arrive, we’re already lit and rolling. Coffee’s on. Most sessions wrap early.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    First cut in about 48 hours, two rounds of changes included, files yours to keep.

Good to know

Questions we get asked.

Can I hire the studio without a crew?

Yes. Studio-only hire is R1 250 per hour and includes the set of your choice, the lighting rig and power. You bring your own cameras and operator. A technician is on site to help you get set up.

How many people can the space hold?

The podcast sets are built for up to four people on camera. For larger shoots, the open floor comfortably takes a crew of twelve plus talent, with a separate lounge area for clients and a green-room corner for talent.

How quickly do we get our files?

Photography selects land in an online gallery the same day, with retouched files in three working days. Video and podcast first cuts are typically ready within 48 hours. Campaign work is scheduled against an agreed timeline.

Do you shoot outside the studio?

Often. The Hub is the base, not the limit — we shoot on location across Gauteng at no extra travel charge, and elsewhere in South Africa with travel quoted upfront.

What do we need to bring?

Yourself, your talking points and anything you want on camera — product, wardrobe, branding. Everything else is here: mics, cameras, lights, backdrops, teleprompter, steamer and wifi.

Can you handle the whole content plan, not just the shoot?

That’s the point of the Hub. iNCO is a full design and media agency, so strategy, scripting, branding, editing and distribution can all sit with one team instead of four.
